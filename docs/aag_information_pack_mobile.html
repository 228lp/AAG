What is AAG?

The Adolescent Advisory Group is a small group of young people aged 10–24 who help us improve a research project about language learning.

You would not be taking part in the research study itself. You would be acting as an advisor by sharing your views on the study and how it should be designed.

What you’ll help with
  • Review study materials and instructions
  • Test online language-learning tasks
  • Share your ideas and opinions on what works well
  • Tell us what feels unclear, boring, difficult, or confusing
  • Suggest ways we could improve things
Time commitment

There will be 4 sessions in total. They are short, online, and designed to be manageable.

Session 1 ~45 mins

Introduction and feedback on materials.

Session 2 ~30 mins

Try online tasks independently. No live meeting needed.

Session 3 ~1 hour

Test tasks and give group feedback.

Session 4 ~1 hour

Final feedback and wrap-up.

Times will be confirmed later.

Who can join?
  • Aged 10–24
  • Currently learning, or who have learned, an additional language
  • Interested in sharing their perspective and experience
No special knowledge is needed — we just want your perspective.
What you’ll get
  • Paid for your time (£96.30 if attending all sessions)
  • Certificate of involvement
  • Useful experience for CVs and applications
  • A chance to help shape real research at Cambridge
Safety & privacy

Your safety and wellbeing are very important to us.

  • The group will be run by a university researcher
  • The facilitator has an enhanced DBS check
  • Clear group rules will be in place
  • Only essential personal information will be collected and stored securely on University of Cambridge systems
  • Your feedback will be anonymised in any reports or outputs
If you are under 18

A parent or guardian will need to give consent, and we will ask them to be present or nearby during sessions.

How to apply

If you are interested, complete the short expression of interest form.

We will select members on a rolling basis, while also aiming for a varied mix of experiences and perspectives across the group.
